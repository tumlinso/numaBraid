# Transport baseline — 2026-08-29

This campaign used the validated physical roles from NB-01 and resolved every
role from its PCI identity. The accepted run is CUDA-controller evidence
`d1c4849c-5bbc-4b85-954b-733d26301bfb`; its complete JSON Lines output is
`2026-08-29-transport.jsonl`.

## Environment and method

- Four Tesla V100-SXM2-16GB GPUs, driver 580.173.02, CUDA runtime/toolkit
  12.9/12.9.86, GCC 13.3.0, and `nvcc -arch=sm_70`.
- Each GPU reported PCIe Gen3 x8 negotiated (Gen3 x16 maximum). Carrier pairs
  A0/A1 and B0/B1 reported six active NVLinks. Same-side pairs reported `PHB`.
- No foreign GPU processes were present. Initial temperatures were 38-40 C;
  clocks were idle before the controlled run and boosted during work.
- Sizes were 4 KiB, 64 KiB, 1 MiB, 16 MiB, and 256 MiB. Every result used five
  warmups and seven batch trials. Reported GB/s is decimal, one-way application
  payload bandwidth based on the median per-copy time.
- P2P-capable legs used a destination-SM scalar pull, a transport pattern also
  validated independently by NVIDIA's `simpleP2P`. Non-P2P diagonals used
  CUDA's runtime peer-copy fallback with explicit completion on both devices.
- Exact byte comparison passed on all 60 direction/size combinations before
  the accepted measurement run.

## Large-transfer results

At 256 MiB, averaging the four directed cases in each class:

| Route class | Mean payload GB/s | Directed range GB/s | Mean time |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Direct NV6 carrier leg | 136.88 | 136.85-136.89 | 1.961 ms |
| Same-NUMA PCIe P2P (`PHB`) | 5.18 | 5.15-5.21 | 51.840 ms |
| Current cross-NUMA CUDA fallback (`SYS`, no P2P) | 5.81 | 5.74-5.87 | 46.217 ms |

The physical bottleneck for a proposed PCIe-then-NVLink route is therefore the
same-NUMA PCIe leg at about 5.18 GB/s, not NVLink. The current cross-NUMA runtime
fallback's effective application bandwidth is similar at large sizes, but it
has no direct CUDA P2P capability and is not evidence that payload avoids the
CPU inter-socket path. NB-03 must establish its actual transport.

At 4 KiB, mean median latency was 4.54 us for direct NVLink, 5.81 us for
same-NUMA PCIe P2P, and 15.81 us for the non-P2P cross-NUMA runtime path. At
1 MiB, the corresponding mean throughputs were 88.16, 5.04, and 2.99 GB/s.

## Diagnostic provenance

An early harness revision lacked an explicit cross-device ordering edge after
source H2D initialization. It produced invalid payload checks and one Xid 31
copy-engine MMU fault on A0 at 15:52:59 UTC; those results were rejected. No
driver reset or topology change was performed. NVIDIA's own `simpleP2P` then
passed 64 MiB correctness on physical A0/A1 and measured 131.88 GB/s under
controller evidence `a90f3c84-e3bc-4920-a37b-5219f5f8499d`. The corrected
numaBraid run completed without any new Xid, with exact-byte validation and
stable repeated measurements.
